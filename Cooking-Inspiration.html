The Familiar Staples

Forks Over KnivesEasy

WFPB, oil-free by default. Huge archive, mostly weeknight-friendly.

The Happy PearEasy

Irish twin brothers, veg-forward, big YouTube presence. Casual, high-energy style, quick meals.

Straight Up FoodEasy

Cathy Fisher. No oil, minimal ingredients, very clear technique — probably the lowest-friction site on this list.

Plant-Based on a BudgetEasy

Cheap, simple, unfussy.

Minimalist BakerEasy

Built around ≤10 ingredients, ≤30 minutes, one bowl/pan. Not all WFPB but easy to adapt.

Rainbow Plant LifeMedium

More flavor-forward and layered than the above, still approachable. More steps, bigger flavor payoff.

Budget BytesEasy

Not vegan-specific but a large, well-tested vegan section with very clear technique instructions.

Newer / Rising Voices

Hermann — Julius FiedlerMedium

Filmmaker-turned-food-creator; researches traditional dishes from cultures worldwide that happen to already be vegan (Keralan pancakes, Turkish köfte, Sicilian street food) rather than veganizing Western comfort food. 2026 cookbook Naturally Vegan. Technique-driven, good for nudging past the current repertoire.

Jeffrey BoadiEasy–Medium

Longtime social-media recipe creator, first cookbook (Plant Fuel) released in 2026. Plant-forward, everyday focus.

Rachel AmaEasy

Caribbean/African-rooted flavor, two bestselling cookbooks built around one-pot meals that work multiple ways across a week. Explicitly designed against elaborate weeknight cooking.

Cookbook Authors Worth Following (strong reviews/sales, last ~2 years)

Gena Hamshaw — The Full HelpingEasy

Registered dietitian; A Grain, a Green, a Bean (2024) is built on one simple repeatable formula rather than a sprawling recipe index — about as low-decision-load as it gets.

The Vegan 8 — Brandi DomingEasy

Every recipe capped at 8 ingredients; newest book is oil-free. Simplicity is the whole premise, similar spirit to Minimalist Baker.

Nisha VoraMedium

2024 book Big Vegan Flavor was James-Beard-nominated; explicitly about technique for home cooks rather than fine dining. Earlier Vegan Instant Pot Cookbook is a long-running favorite.

Joanne Lee Molinaro — The Korean VeganMedium

Second book (Homemade) is looser and more personal than her James-Beard-winning debut; Koreanized takes on American/Italian/Mexican comfort food. Not oil-free but adaptable.
